The seam problem

Every handoff is a transfer, and every transfer is paperwork.

Press at one license holder. Package at a second. Move it with a third. Every handoff is a change of custody, and every change of custody means a manifest, a transport window and often a re-test. None of that is processing. It is the cost of the seams.

Wildseed holds both licenses, so your material stays with one operator. Fewer handoffs, less paperwork, and fewer places for a schedule to slip.

The toll run

Five steps. Each one has a document attached.

Toll processing is a sequence. This is the order it happens in.

01

Intake and quote

Tell us what you have and what you want back. We send a written quote and a work order.

02

Transfer

Your material travels on a METRC manifest, in your vehicle or ours. Title stays with you.

03

Processing

Your lot runs on its own, tracked by source. We never blend it with anyone else.

04

Testing

We book the lab. Results come back attached to your batch record.

05

Return or fulfill

Take the finished product back, or we deliver it into licensed retail ourselves.

What’s on the floor

Five services, one roof, one license holder.

Take one of them or take all five. Adding a second does not add a vendor, a manifest or a handoff. It is the same license holder, in the same building.

01

Vape cart filling

Filling, capping and finishing 1g and 0.5g carts under your brand, from distillate, live resin or rosin inputs. Hardware to your spec or ours, with batch documentation on every run.

02

Rosin toll processing

Solventless processing of your fresh frozen or dry material into press-ready hash and finished rosin. Micron selection to spec, cold cure available, single-source runs kept separate.

03

Flower and pre-rolls

Bulk flower into retail-ready eighths, jars, pre-rolls and multi-packs, with DCC-compliant labeling applied in-house before the batch leaves the floor.

04

Distribution

Statewide transport on our distribution license, with METRC manifesting and lab-testing coordination. Accepted as a standalone last-mile service.

05

Cold storage

Licensed freezer capacity for fresh frozen material through the gap between harvest and press, with lot-level segregation. Storage-only accepted.

The line

Category level, by design: no SKU names, no strain designations, no potencies, no prices. What each format is, and how it is made, is below.

01 · 8 SKUs

Solventless rosin

Pressed from single-source fresh frozen and cold cured. Solventless means the resin is separated with heat and pressure instead of a hydrocarbon or ethanol wash; single-source means the lot behind a press came from one source rather than a blend; a cold cure is a low-temperature hold after pressing rather than a heated one.

Why we run it

Same floor as your run.

We run our own line on the same equipment we run yours on. It’s the reason we know what the process does to a terpene profile before you ask.

There is no second, quieter operation behind this label. The press, the cure and the filling bench are the ones a client run uses, so when we tell you how a cold cure will read on a single-source fresh frozen lot, we’re describing equipment we operate ourselves rather than a vendor’s spec sheet.

It also means we are a customer of our own process. Anything that would go wrong on your lot has already had the chance to go wrong on ours.

About Wildseed.

At Wildseed, cannabis isn’t just a business. It is a craft, a community, and a legacy. With roots that span from Arcata to LA and over 25 years of California cannabis expertise, we’re built for excellence, innovation, and authenticity.

Cannastewards
